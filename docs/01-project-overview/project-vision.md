# Project Vision — AI Financial Research Terminal

## 1. Executive Summary

The **AI Financial Research Terminal** is a full-stack, enterprise-grade equity research platform designed to bridge the gap between deterministic financial numbers and qualitative disclosures. It empowers equity analysts, investment researchers, and retail investors to analyze public companies with the speed of an institutional terminal and the analytical depth of an AI research assistant.

The platform combines:
1. **Deterministic Financial Analytics**: Hard financial figures (Revenue, EBITDA, Net Profit, EPS, Debt-to-Equity, Operating Margins) calculated through verifiable backend algorithms and served via PostgreSQL.
2. **Context-Grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: An AI analyst that answers qualitative questions ("Why did operating margins compress?", "What is management's guidance on FMCG demand?") using official regulatory filings and earnings call transcripts with precise citations.
3. **Primary Source Integrity**: Clear attribution that separates verified financial facts from management guidance, analyst questions, and executive commentary.

---

## 2. Core Value Proposition

| Traditional Problem | AI Terminal Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hallucination in Standard AI**: LLMs invent numbers or confuse fiscal quarters when answering financial queries. | **Separation of Concerns**: Numerical data is computed deterministically in Express & PostgreSQL; the LLM only synthesizes extracted qualitative evidence. |
| **Opaque Answers**: Generic chatbots give opinions without citing specific documents or pages. | **Verifiable Citations**: Every answer provides exact source links, PDF page numbers, transcript speakers, and sections. |
| **Transcript Blindspot**: Standard tools focus on numbers, ignoring the richest source of strategic context: conference call Q&As. | **Transcripts as First-Class Citizens**: Full speaker parsing (CEO, CFO, Analyst) distinguishing prepared remarks from critical Q&A pushback. |
| **Fragmented Workflow**: Investors switch between stock charts, 200-page PDF annual reports, and transcript transcripts. | **Unified Research Workspace**: Single terminal dashboard with charts, financials, document viewer, and conversational AI panel. |

---

## 3. Target Audience

1. **Equity Research Associates & Analysts**: Who need to rapidly synthesize quarterly earnings commentary across fiscal quarters.
2. **Individual Investors**: Seeking institutional-grade research capabilities without navigating convoluted regulatory databases.
3. **Academic & Portfolio Evaluators**: Serving as a demonstration of production-grade software architecture, Agile delivery, data integrity, and responsible AI engineering.

---

## 4. Product Guiding Principles

1. **Numbers Come From Code, Not LLMs**: Financial ratios and metrics must never be generated by LLM prompts. They are calculated deterministically.
2. **Attribution is Mandatory**: Management claims must be phrased as management statements (*"During the Q4 call, the CFO stated..."*), not objective absolute truths.
3. **Zero Fabrications**: If evidence does not exist in the ingested document corpus, the system explicitly states that the information is unavailable.
4. **Clean Decoupling**: Separation of main application logic (Express.js) from RAG processing (Python/FastAPI) to ensure scalability, clean boundary management, and testability.
