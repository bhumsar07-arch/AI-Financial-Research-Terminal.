=== Prepared Remarks ===

Operator: Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. Welcome to the ITC Limited Q4 and Full Year FY24 Earnings Conference Call. Today's call is being recorded. I now hand the conference over to Mr. Sanjiv Puri, Chairman and Managing Director of ITC Limited. Thank you, and over to you, sir.

Sanjiv Puri - Chairman and Managing Director: Thank you, operator. Good evening to everyone joining us on the call today. I am pleased to share that ITC has delivered another year of resilient performance in FY2024 amidst a macroeconomic environment characterized by persistent food inflation, geopolitical volatility, and subdued rural demand. Our gross revenue for the full year crossed 76,800 Crores, and Profit After Tax crossed 20,400 Crores, reflecting the sustained structural strength of our diversified business portfolio.

In our FMCG-Others business, we delivered competitive revenue growth despite severe inflationary pressures on agricultural raw materials. Our market-leading brands such as Aashirvaad, Sunfeast, Bingo!, and Yippee! continued to expand their consumer franchise through differentiated innovation and digital distribution. On the proposed demerger of the Hotels business, the scheme of arrangement has received strong shareholder approval and is progressing through statutory regulatory approvals. We remain committed to asset-right growth and value creation for all stakeholders. I will now ask our CFO, Mr. Supratim Dutta, to take you through the detailed financial drivers.

Supratim Dutta - Chief Financial Officer: Thank you, Chairman. Good evening, everyone. Let me provide specific color on our operating margins and segment performance for the full year and fourth quarter. 

Our overall Operating Profit for FY24 reached 24,800 Crores, reflecting an operating margin of approximately 32.3%. However, profitability across segments experienced diverging drivers. In our Cigarettes segment, stable taxation regimes enabled sustained volume recovery and premiumization, delivering steady margin expansion. 

Conversely, our Paperboards, Paper and Packaging segment faced significant headwinds throughout FY24. Segment margins contracted sharply due to two simultaneous factors: first, a sharp surge in domestic wood costs driven by unseasonal weather and heightened demand from wood-based industries; and second, aggressive low-priced dumping of paperboards into India from China and Indonesia following global supply-demand imbalances. 

In our Agri-Business, while revenue was impacted by Government export restrictions on food commodities—specifically wheat, non-basmati rice, and onion exports aimed at domestic food security—our strategic focus on value-added agricultural products including spices, coffee, and customized specialty crops helped cushion the overall margin impact.

=== Question-and-Answer Session ===

Operator: Thank you very much. We will now begin the question-and-answer session. The first question comes from the line of Percy Panthaki from CLSA. Please go ahead.

Percy Panthaki - Analyst, CLSA: Good evening, Sanjiv and Supratim. My first question is on the Agri-Business. Can you explain why operating profit margins were lower in Agri-Business during FY24, and what is your outlook on commodity export bans for the upcoming fiscal year?

Supratim Dutta - Chief Financial Officer: Thanks, Percy. As highlighted in our remarks, the primary driver for the moderation in Agri-Business trading revenue was indeed the government-mandated export bans on non-basmati white rice and wheat. These bans directly restricted our high-volume bulk grain merchanting operations. However, our value-added portfolio—particularly certified sustainable spices, coffee, and organic food ingredients—grew at double digits with healthy margins. Going into FY25, while we do not speculate on government trade policy, we are structurally insulating the business by expanding our integrated value chains and deep farmer backward linkages through the ITC MAARS digital ecosystem.

Operator: Thank you. The next question comes from the line of Vivek Maheshwari from Jefferies. Please go ahead.

Vivek Maheshwari - Analyst, Jefferies: Good evening. My question is for Sanjiv on the cigarette business. We have seen volume resilience over the last six quarters. Do you anticipate this volume momentum to continue, and how are you managing illicit cigarette competition?

Sanjiv Puri - Chairman and Managing Director: Thank you, Vivek. The single most important enabler of volume stability in the legal cigarette industry is predictable and stable taxation. In periods where excise duty and tax rates remain calibrated, consumers shift back from the illicit, duty-evaded market to legitimate, tax-paid brands. We have reinforced this tailwind by driving localized product innovations, capsule formats, and expanding our direct retail touchpoints. As long as fiscal policy remains calibrated, we are confident in sustaining positive volume trajectories.

Operator: Thank you. The next question comes from the line of Amit Sachdeva from HSBC. Please go ahead.

Amit Sachdeva - Analyst, HSBC: Hi, good evening team. I wanted to follow up on the Paperboards segment margin contraction. You mentioned wood price spikes and Chinese imports. How long do you expect domestic wood cost inflation to persist, and have you engaged with the government regarding import duties on paperboards?

Supratim Dutta - Chief Financial Officer: Hi Amit. Regarding domestic wood availability: domestic wood prices touched record highs during the middle of FY24 due to supply shortages. In response, ITC has aggressively accelerated its agro-forestry plantations, planting over 1.1 million acres to date, which will progressively increase captive wood availability over the medium term. On the import front, through industry associations like the Indian Paper Manufacturers Association (IPMA), representations have been made to the Directorate General of Trade Remedies (DGTR) regarding unfair dumping of subsidized paperboard into India under ASEAN FTAs. We expect some normalization in wood raw material inflation in the second half of FY25.

Operator: Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, that concludes the question-and-answer session. I now hand the conference back to management for closing comments.

Sanjiv Puri - Chairman and Managing Director: Thank you all for your engagement, insightful questions, and continued support for ITC Limited. We remain steadfast in delivering durable, sustainable value across our diversified portfolio. Good evening.
